A robotics fulfilment company runs about 3,000 Linux-brained machines and their
supporting infrastructure across three regions. The robots are edge-gateway
class nodes: small Linux boxes with cellular uplinks, monitored for their
processes, CPU, memory, disks and radios.

MOBILE ROBOTS (owned by the robotics team; every robot carries region,
function, fleet and criticality labels):

- 800 gen-3 picker AMRs at the eu-west-1 fulfilment centre:
  region=eu-west-1, function=picker, fleet=gen3, criticality=critical
- 400 gen-3 picker AMRs at the us-east-1 centre:
  region=us-east-1, function=picker, fleet=gen3, criticality=critical
- 300 gen-2 forklift AMRs in eu-west-1:
  region=eu-west-1, function=forklift, fleet=gen2, criticality=standard
- 200 gen-2 forklift AMRs in eu-central-1:
  region=eu-central-1, function=forklift, fleet=gen2, criticality=standard
- 200 inspection robots in eu-west-1:
  region=eu-west-1, function=inspection, fleet=gen3, criticality=batch
- 120 inspection robots in eu-central-1:
  region=eu-central-1, function=inspection, fleet=gen3, criticality=batch
- 80 inspection robots in us-east-1:
  region=us-east-1, function=inspection, fleet=gen3, criticality=batch
- 300 stationary robot arms in the eu-central-1 assembly cells:
  region=eu-central-1, function=arm, fleet=gen2, criticality=critical
- 200 stationary robot arms in us-east-1:
  region=us-east-1, function=arm, fleet=gen2, criticality=critical

SITES (owned by the networks team):

- 20 edge gateways in eu-west-1 that aggregate robot traffic:
  region=eu-west-1, function=gateway, team=networks
- 12 edge gateways in eu-central-1:
  region=eu-central-1, function=gateway, team=networks
- 8 edge gateways in us-east-1:
  region=us-east-1, function=gateway, team=networks
- 12 managed Cisco Catalyst access switches in eu-west-1:
  region=eu-west-1, function=network, team=networks
- 6 managed Cisco Catalyst access switches in eu-central-1:
  region=eu-central-1, function=network, team=networks
- 6 managed Cisco Catalyst access switches in us-east-1:
  region=us-east-1, function=network, team=networks
- 2 core routers per region (6 total):
  region=<the region they are in>, function=core, team=networks

FLEET-MANAGEMENT PLATFORM (owned by the platform team, split evenly across the
three regions; each node carries region and team=platform and its tier):

- 2 HAProxy load balancers per region (6 total) fronting the application:
  tier=frontend
- 12 nginx web nodes per region (36 total) running the fleet-management
  application: tier=frontend
- 4 Redis caches per region (12 total) for robot state and sessions:
  tier=cache

DATA TIER (owned by the data team; each node carries region and team=data):

- 4 PostgreSQL nodes per region (12 total: a primary, a replica and two
  analytics nodes per region): tier=database

ORCHESTRATION (owned by the platform team; region and team=platform labels):

- 3 Kubernetes control-plane nodes per region (9 total): tier=orchestration
- 15 Kubernetes workers per region (45 total) running the robot orchestration
  and telemetry stack: tier=orchestration

Every node carries its region and its team; robots additionally carry
function, fleet and criticality as listed above.
